Straight talk

A killer homepage. Not the whole website.

LinQ Scroll Stack builds the scroll: a cinematic homepage or landing page that makes people stop. A complete business website needs a lot more behind it. We'd rather you hear that from us than from a client.

What this kit builds

The page that wins the click

  • A homepage or landing page with an AI hero film that plays as you scroll
  • A phone layout that holds up, not a shrunk desktop
  • On-page SEO for that page: title, description and schema
  • The page live on a link you can send a client today
A full website also needs

Everything behind the homepage

  • Interior pages: services, about, service areas, contact, blog
  • The code in GitHub, on hosting the client owns
  • Forms connected to a real tool like Formspree or GoHighLevel, so every lead lands somewhere
  • Pipelines and automations on those forms: CRM stages, texts, emails, booking
  • Site-wide SEO: sitemap, internal links, local pages, Search Console, Google Business Profile
  • Analytics and tracking, speed, accessibility and ongoing upkeep
